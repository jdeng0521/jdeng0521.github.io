About

I’m a master’s student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Johns Hopkins University. I received my bachelor’s degree in Automation from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

My research has taken me from reconstructing deformable tissue in endoscopic video to fusing aerial and roadside observations for 3D object detection.

Research Interests

I’m interested in 3D computer vision, robotics, and deep learning. My work has focused on spatial perception: how to combine evidence across viewpoints and time, and how to reconstruct scenes when the camera, objects, or the scene itself is moving.

I’ve explored these questions through collaborative 3D detection, deformable simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM), and 3D occupancy prediction for autonomous driving.

Selected Research

Air–Ground Collaborative 3D Detection

Selective fusion of aerial, roadside, and historical observations for 3D object detection on the AirV2X benchmark.

Deformable Endoscopic SLAM

Camera tracking and dense reconstruction of deforming tissue using 3D Gaussian splatting.
